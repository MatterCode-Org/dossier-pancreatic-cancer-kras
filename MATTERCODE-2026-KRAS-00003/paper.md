# Explicit-Solvent OpenMM Validation of KRAS G12D Lead MATTERCODE-2026-KRAS-00003: MM-GBSA Binding Free Energy and Retrosynthetic Accessibility

**Paper ID:** MC-2026-KRAS-PAP-00003

## Abstract

We report physics-audited validation of MATTERCODE-2026-KRAS-00003 against KRAS G12D (PDB 7ZCG) using OpenMM explicit-solvent MD.

## 2 Methods

Explicit-solvent MD with OpenMM 8.2 (Amber14SB, GAFF2/AM1-BCC, TIP3P, 4.0 fs HMR) on a dedicated RTX 5090 physics node.
